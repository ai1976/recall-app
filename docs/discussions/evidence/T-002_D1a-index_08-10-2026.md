## 4. Claude's reading (analysis, NOT evidence; for QA to audit)
**Catalogue facts (VERIFIED in the saved JSON):** `plpgsql`, `SECURITY DEFINER`, owner `postgres`, volatility `v` (volatile), configuration `search_path=public, extensions`, EXECUTE held by `authenticated`, `postgres`, `service_role` (not `anon`, not PUBLIC). Flags: insert, update, delete, truncate, merge, copy, DDL word, `set_config`/`set role`, `perform`/`call`, dynamic `EXECUTE` are **all false**; `language_unresolved` false. Leads: routine `is_admin`; relations `flashcards`, `reviews`, `subjects`. Nothing in the text can write, so a read-only role-bound call (D1b) is not blocked by what the body does; it is blocked only by the open `is_admin` lead.

**What the body does (read from the saved definition):**
1. Access guard: raises unless `p_user_id` equals `auth.uid()` or `is_admin()`.
2. `available_cards`: every flashcard with `(p_course_level IS NULL OR target_course = p_course_level)`, `(visibility = 'public' OR user_id = p_user_id)`, excluding concept cards. **A NULL course parameter means every course.**
3. `latest_reviews`: the user's latest `active` review per card (not restricted to a course); it then joins to `available_cards`.
4. Per subject: `total_cards` = cards in `available_cards`; `reviewed_count` = those with a review; `mastery_pct` = share with latest quality of at least 3; `due_count` = latest reviews with `next_review_date::date <= CURRENT_DATE` and `skip_until` null or earlier than `CURRENT_DATE`.

**Facts of the text that bear on the open observation (not a cause):**
- It does not read `my_cards_enrollment` at all (a card archived, removed or paused through enrollment is judged only by its review row).
- "Today" is `CURRENT_DATE`, the database session date, not the student's local date that the shared due helper (C-01) uses.
- `total_cards` counts every public card of the course plus the student's own, whether or not the student is enrolled in it.
- With `p_course_level` NULL the table lists subjects across all courses.
- So its `due_count` is a **different definition of due** from the one now shared by the badge, the Progress tiles and the Dashboard (T-001 slice 1).

**HYPOTHESIS, to be tested by D1b and not stated as a cause:** the earlier observation (seven subjects, Business Laws with 24 due, shown under CA Intermediate after a course round trip) would be explained if the table was called with a NULL course at some moment (`SubjectMasteryTable.jsx` passes `courseLevel ?? null`), because a NULL parameter lists every course. D1b already includes the NULL case and the two other platform courses; its independent expected result is what decides.
