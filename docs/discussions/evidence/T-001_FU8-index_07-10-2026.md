- **E4 and decision D3 (batch groups survive the deletion of their creator).** **`study_groups.created_by` is NOT NULL**, and `content_group_shares.shared_by`, `study_group_members.user_id` and `batch_group_professors.professor_id` (part of the primary key) are NOT NULL too, so the existing `ON DELETE CASCADE` to `profiles` cannot simply become `SET NULL` on `created_by`: letting a batch group survive needs a **different mechanism** (for example reassigning the creator to a platform-owned identity before the profile is removed, or a reviewed change of the column to nullable with the foreign key changed to `SET NULL`, plus handling of the other NOT NULL actor columns).
