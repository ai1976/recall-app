**G4 (course tables, triggers, indexes, foreign keys):**
- **Triggers:** only three triggers exist on the six inspected tables, all on `profiles` and enabled: `trg_badge_new_profile`, `trg_course_change_archive_restore`, `trg_guard_profiles_protected_columns`. **The profile guard trigger is attached live** (resolves that open item). There are **no triggers** on `disciplines`, `subjects`, `access_requests`, `study_group_members` or `study_groups`.
- **Indexes:** `disciplines` has only its primary key (no unique name, confirmed live); `subjects` has only its primary key (so the composite foreign key of brief B needs a new unique pair); `access_requests` has only the primary key and two plain indexes (no unique or partial indexes); `study_groups` has no unique index on `invite_token`; `study_group_members` has the unique (`group_id`, `user_id`) index.
- **Privileges:** `anon` and `authenticated` hold SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE on `disciplines`, `subjects` and `topics`. RLS is enabled on these tables (F2), but the policies on them were not captured, so whether a client can actually write them is OPEN.
- **Foreign keys:** `flashcards.subject_id` and `notes.subject_id` reference `subjects` with no delete action (restrict), so a subject that cards or notes use cannot be deleted; `flashcard_decks.subject_id` is `ON DELETE SET NULL` (deleting a subject silently clears the deck's subject); `topics.subject_id` and `subjects.discipline_id` and `profile_courses.discipline_id` are `ON DELETE CASCADE`; `flashcards.discipline_id` and `notes.discipline_id` reference `disciplines` with no delete action. Deleting a discipline cascades to its subjects, which is blocked in practice only while cards or notes reference those subjects.
