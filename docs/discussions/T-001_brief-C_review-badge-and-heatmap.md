## 1. What the Founder sees today, in plain words
1. **The red Review badge does not update** after you Pause, Resume, Remove or Re-add a card, finish a review, or switch course; it is only correct after a full reload. (After Pause or Remove the number should fall; after Resume or Re-add it may rise. The defect is that it does not change at all.)
2. **The heatmap shows one number per day for all study time, offline and in-app mixed,** in a native hover title only. It cannot be reached by keyboard, and a phone cannot show it at all.

## 2. What is already agreed (status block of the thread, 02/10/2026; positions, not an approval)

- Point 7: split offline (`study_sessions.source = 'manual'`) from in-app (`study_mode`, `practice_mode`); an accessible hover and focus tooltip plus a pinned tap detail.

## 4. Design (PROPOSAL)
### Point 6: one meaning of "due", and a badge that stays correct
**C-6.1 The shared eligibility rule (one definition).** A card counts as "due-eligible" for a student when **all** hold, evaluated against `v_today`, the student's local date from `profiles.timezone`: (a) `reviews.status = 'active'`; (b) `skip_until IS NULL OR skip_until <= v_today`; (c) not a `concept_card`; (d) the course rule of the live predicate; (e) the visibility rule of the live predicate; (f) **new:** an **active** `my_cards_enrollment` row for the card (the exact boundary of `get_my_cards`). The student's "due" counts are all taken from this one set.

**C-6.2 The day-zero invariant and the future-bucket contract (these are different and separate).**
- **Day zero (badge, Dashboard "Reviews due", Progress "due today"):** the count equals the number of cards the Review page lets the student review today, i.e. `|get_study_queue ∩ get_my_cards|`, and equals `get_due_forecast.due_today` and bucket 0 of `get_due_forecast_buckets`. This is an equality, tested for **every profile** (C-6.5).
- **Future buckets (Forward Load) and `due_next_7` / `due_next_30`:** they describe the *same eligible set* spread by scheduled date; they are **not** "what Review permits today". Rule: the set is the C-6.1 set, with the skip boundary (b) **kept exactly as live, evaluated against `v_today` for every bucket** (so a card whose `skip_until` is still in the future is excluded from every bucket, including buckets dated after its skip date; this is the live behaviour, preserved on purpose, recorded here as a known simplification and not changed by this brief). Invariants: for every profile, the sum of all eight buckets equals the number of C-6.1-eligible cards with no date limit; `due_next_7` and `due_next_30` equal the count of that set with `next_review_date <= v_today + 7` and `+ 30`; bucket 0 equals `due_today`.

**C-6.3 One shared implementation boundary and a parity test.** One internal helper (name and exact form fixed at the SQL stage) holds the C-6.1 rule, and **both** `get_due_forecast` and `get_due_forecast_buckets` read from it, so they cannot drift from each other. The Review page's composition (`get_study_queue` intersected with `get_my_cards`) is **not** changed; an all-profile parity test (C-6.5) compares it with the helper, so any later change to either side fails the test.

**C-6.4 The refresh contract (one behaviour, one place).**
- *Single source in the app.* The nav context fetches `get_due_forecast` and `get_due_forecast_buckets` **together** and exposes `dueToday`, `dueNext7`, `dueNext30` and the buckets; the nav badge, the Dashboard "Reviews due" and Forward Load chart and the Progress "due today" tile **read from that context** and stop running their own due queries. All surfaces are therefore always in the same state.
- *One invalidation signal.* The context exposes one function, `notifyReviewDataChanged()`; calling it refetches both RPCs. It is called: (1) after a **successful** Pause, Resume, Remove or Re-add on My Cards; (2) after each graded answer **has been saved successfully** in a review session, **debounced** with a trailing delay of 1.5 seconds so rapid answers coalesce into one refetch, plus once when the session ends or the page is left; (3) after a **successful** course switch; (4) when the tab becomes visible again after more than 60 seconds (covers review activity on another device). No polling. A failed action does not call it.
- *Only the badge's data is held centrally;* Review-page queue logic is unchanged.

**C-6.5 Proof for point 6 (contracts above, each tested; read-only comparisons run over ALL profiles, never a sample).**
1. **Day-zero equality, every profile, zero exceptions:** `due_today = bucket 0 = |get_study_queue ∩ get_my_cards|`. **This includes the account with the 59 no-enrollment cards: those cards no longer count, so the mismatch for that account must also be 0.** The unexplained origin of their missing enrollment rows may stay OPEN; the invariant may not.
2. **Future-bucket reconciliation, every profile:** the three invariants of C-6.2.
3. **Explicit boundary cases (real-role TEST):** `reviews.status` active, paused, mastered, suspended; enrollment active, removed, none; own, public, friends-accepted and friends-pending visibility; `concept_card` excluded; course null, equal, different; `skip_until` yesterday, today, tomorrow (skip boundary), with a card dated today and a card dated in 3 days; another user's rows never counted; a user in a time zone at +14 and at −12 on the local-date edge.
4. **Authorization:** the caller must be the target or `is_admin()` (unchanged); `anon` refused; grants not widened (compared with RUN 2).
5. **Live check by the Founder:** the badge, the Dashboard number, the Progress tile and the Forward Load "Today" bar agree and change within about two seconds after each of: Pause, Resume, Remove, Re-add, answering a card, switching course.

**C-6.6 The own cards without an enrollment row (OPEN, Founder decision D-C2).** After C-6.1 the 59 (or 103) cards leave every due count (the Review page already hides them) and remain unreviewable until an enrollment exists. Whether to leave them, enroll them by a one-off reviewed data fix, or first find how own cards end up without an enrollment (the SQL path for batch-created cards) is a separate decision; this brief does not repair data.

### Point 7: an honest, accessible heatmap
**C-7.1 The data contract (new RPC; the old one stays until the frontend is verified).** Returns, per local date: `review_count` (computed exactly as live, unchanged), `in_app_seconds` (`study_mode` plus `practice_mode`), `offline_seconds` (`manual`), `study_seconds` (the live total over **all** sources), and `other_seconds` = `study_seconds − in_app_seconds − offline_seconds`, which must be 0 for every day (it exists so that any source outside the three known ones fails a test instead of disappearing). In-app plus offline equals the total whenever `other_seconds` is 0.
**C-7.2 Authorization and grants (preserved, not widened).** Caller must be the target or `is_admin()`, exactly as the live function (P7-E1), because an administrator may legitimately read a student's heatmap today; the new function is `SECURITY DEFINER` with the same pinned `search_path`, `anon` refused, and grants no wider than the live function's (RUN 2). If the Founder wants administrator access removed, that is a separate decision (D-C4, recommended: keep as live).
**C-7.3 Day and window contract.** (a) Review days: grouped by the profile timezone date, as live. (b) Study days: `session_date` as written by the client (the student's local date); the function does not re-derive it. (c) **The window start is the student's local today (from `profiles.timezone`, default `Asia/Kolkata`) minus `p_days`, for all three sources,** replacing the live mix of `NOW()` and the server's `CURRENT_DATE`, so the edge is correct at both positive and negative UTC offsets. (d) The known limit that a student whose device date differs from their profile timezone can see a one-day difference between review days and study days is recorded and not changed here.
**C-7.4 Frontend contract.** Dates from the server are calendar-date strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`); the component never calls `new Date('YYYY-MM-DD')` (UTC) and never `toISOString()`: it splits the string into year, month and day and builds a local date, or keeps the string as the cell key; the grid is built from local dates with `toLocaleDateString('en-CA')`. Each cell is a focusable control with an accessible name (date, in-app, offline, total, reviews; not colour alone); hover and keyboard focus show a concise card (date, in-app time, offline time, total, reviews); tap, Enter or Space **pins** the detail, which closes on Escape, a second tap or focus loss; the native `title` is removed; reduced-motion is respected; the existing visual language is kept.
**C-7.5 Proof for point 7 (read-only reconciliation over all profiles, plus real-role TEST).** (1) For every profile and day, `in_app + offline + other = study_seconds`, `other = 0`, and the new totals equal the old function's `study_seconds` and `review_count`; (2) own rows only; **another student's read refused, an administrator's read allowed, `anon` refused**; (3) boundary cases: a study-only day, a review-only day, an empty day, a day with all three sources, a user at +14 and at −12 on the window edge; (4) frontend: keyboard-only walk-through, screen-reader name of each cell, pin and close behaviour, a negative-UTC browser time zone, no `toISOString()` or UTC string parsing left in the component.
**C-7.6 Not here.** Course and subject in a day-detail view (brief B C4), in-app session classification, professor views (totals only, unchanged).
